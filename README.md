# Dot & Meow

Dot & Meow is a local co-op 2D platformer where two players work together to escape an underground laboratory. Solve puzzles, avoid traps, and cooperate to reach the exit.


## Gameplay Overview

- **2 Players**: Ladybug (Dot) and Cat (Meow)  
- **Goal**: Collect enough points and cooperate to open the final door and escape  
- **Features**:
  - Pressure switches that react to players or objects
  - Color-coded puzzle balls and moving platforms
  - Lethal traps like arrows, ice, and water
  - Checkpoints and death animations
  - Dynamic camera and fade transitions
  - Victory and Game Over screens
  - A lot of nice audio effects


## Controls

| Player | Move         | Jump          |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| Dot    | A / D        | W             |
| Meow   | Left / Right | Up Arrow      |

Note: Game requires two players on the same keyboard.


This project was created for educational purposes as part of a university class.
